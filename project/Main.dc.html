Stage 06 · Onboarding Call

Onboarding SOP

Download SOP
Overview

How we onboard a new client from the moment they verbally agree on the sales call through to the handoff into delivery. The aim is a smooth, professional start where the contract is signed, the invoice is paid, all access is in, and the client sees we are already working before day one.

KPITime-to-launch: days from verbal yes to the start of delivery work.
TargetWork begins within 3 days of the onboarding call, once payment clears.
TriggerClient says yes on the sales call.
Step 01 · On the sales call (the moment they say yes)

Set the next steps clearly and confidently while you have them on the call.

  • Book the onboarding call live on the call, 72 hours out. This is a strict rule: never end the sales call without the onboarding call booked, and never sooner than 72 hours, so there is time to prepare the research and benchmarking.
  • Make clear the next steps, the purpose of the onboarding call, and what happens after it.
  • Explain what they will receive right after this call: the contract, the invoice, and the access documents.
  • Make one thing clear: work starts once the invoice is paid.
Step 02 · Right after the sales call (same day)

Send everything while the momentum is fresh.

  • Send the client the contract, invoice, and the SOP documents for giving access to the necessary platforms.
Step 03 · Between the calls (the 3 days): prepare the material

This is where we show up already working. What you prepare depends on the project type.

  • Prepare sufficient research and data for the onboarding call, such as market research, benchmarking reports, draft campaign structure, or whatever fits the project type.
Step 04 · The onboarding call

The call has one job: confirm everything is in place and show the client the plan so they leave confident and clear on what happens next.

  • Confirm the invoice is paid and the contract is signed. If either is outstanding, sort it on the call and remind them work starts once payment clears.
  • Confirm all access we requested has been granted.
  • Walk the client through their starting point and make sure they understand it, whether that is their visibility %, current rankings, or any other baseline, so both sides agree on where we are starting from.
  • Present the next steps based on the research we have done.
  • Agree a weekly meeting and reporting cadence.
